# Glossary

This glossary defines the key terms used throughout the Dingwei Battery technical documentation.

## Electrical Terms

- **CCA (Cold Cranking Amps)** — the current a battery can deliver for 30 seconds at a defined cold temperature before dropping below a cut-off voltage. See [CCA Testing](../quality/cca-testing.md).
- **Ah (Amp-hour)** — a measure of battery capacity; the current a battery can deliver over a defined period. See [Capacity & Reserve](../quality/capacity-reserve.md).
- **RC (Reserve Capacity)** — the number of minutes a battery can deliver a defined current before dropping below cut-off voltage.
- **C20 Rate** — the standard 20-hour discharge rate for capacity measurement.

## Battery Types

- **Flooded (wet cell)** — traditional lead-acid battery with liquid electrolyte.
- **AGM (Absorbent Glass Mat)** — a lead-acid battery where the electrolyte is absorbed in a glass mat, enabling higher performance and spill resistance.
- **EFB (Enhanced Flooded Battery)** — an improved flooded battery for start-stop vehicles.

## Standards & Sizing

- **JIS** — Japanese Industrial Standard (N-series group sizes).
- **DIN** — Deutsches Institut für Normung (European size classes).
- **BCI** — Battery Council International (North American group sizes).
- **SAE** — Society of Automotive Engineers (−18°C CCA test).
- **EN** — European Norm (−18°C CCA test).
- **Group Size** — a standard classification defining a battery's physical dimensions and terminal layout.

## Manufacturing Terms

- **OEM** — Original Equipment Manufacturing; building to the buyer's specification.
- **ODM** — Original Design Manufacturing; branding an existing manufacturer design.
- **Private Label** — applying your brand to a manufacturer's product.
- **MOQ** — Minimum Order Quantity.
- **Lead Time** — the total time from confirmed order to delivery.

## Quality Terms

- **IATF 16949** — automotive quality management system standard.
- **ISO 9001** — general quality management system standard.
- **ISO 45001 / OHSAS 18001** — occupational health and safety standards.
- **COA (Certificate of Analysis)** — a document certifying a product's tested specifications.
