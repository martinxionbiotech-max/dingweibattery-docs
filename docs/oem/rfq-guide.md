# RFQ Guide

A complete request for quotation (RFQ) gets you a faster, more accurate quote. A vague RFQ forces manufacturers to ask clarifying questions, adding delay. This guide covers the information you should include.

## Essential Information

### 1. Battery Specification
- Standard (JIS, DIN, BCI, SAE)
- Group size or model number
- Voltage and CCA requirement
- Capacity (Ah) and reserve capacity (RC)
- Terminal type and polarity

### 2. Volume & Delivery
- Target annual volume or quantity per order
- Shipping destination (port or address)
- Required delivery timeline
- Recurring vs one-time order

### 3. Branding Requirements
- OEM (your specification) vs private label (your brand)
- Label and packaging requirements
- Custom case or terminal requirements

### 4. Documentation
- Required test documentation
- Certificate of Analysis (COA) requirements
- Compliance certifications needed for your market

## What to Ask For

- Firm MOQ per model
- Production lead time
- Sample availability and timeline
- Payment and shipping terms
- Batch documentation format

## Why Completeness Matters

A complete RFQ lets the manufacturer quote accurately on the first pass, reducing back-and-forth. It also signals that you are a serious buyer, which can improve pricing and priority.
