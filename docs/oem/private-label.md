# Private Label Battery Guide

Private label lets you sell batteries under your own brand without investing in manufacturing infrastructure. Dingwei Battery produces the batteries; you supply the brand and distribution.

## The Private Label Process

1. **Choose your models** — select from existing JIS/DIN/BCI models or specify custom
2. **Design your brand** — labels, colors, packaging, and product naming
3. **Approve samples** — review branded pre-production samples
4. **Set your MOQ** — establish minimum order quantities per model
5. **Place orders** — production runs with your branding
6. **Receive documentation** — batch test data and compliance documents

## What You Can Brand

- Product labels and casing
- Packaging and retail boxes
- Product naming and model codes
- Marketing materials (using manufacturer-provided base specs)

## Strategy Considerations

- **Start with proven models** — begin with standard JIS/DIN models before custom specifications
- **Differentiate on service** — documentation, support, and delivery reliability
- **Confirm compliance** — ensure your market's labeling and regulatory requirements are met

## Building Your Brand

Private label is a faster, lower-risk path to battery brand ownership than building your own factory. The key is choosing a manufacturer whose quality framework (certifications, testing, documentation) backs your brand's claims.
