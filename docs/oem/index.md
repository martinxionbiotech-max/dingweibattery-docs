# OEM Battery Programs

OEM (Original Equipment Manufacturing) means Dingwei Battery manufactures batteries to your specification, branded with your company's identity. This is distinct from buying off-the-shelf batteries.

## How OEM Manufacturing Works

1. **Specification** — you define the battery's electrical, dimensional, and branding requirements
2. **Sampling** — the manufacturer produces pre-production samples for your approval
3. **Testing** — samples are verified against your specification (CCA, capacity, dimensions)
4. **Production** — mass production runs to the approved specification
5. **Quality** — batch testing and documentation throughout

## What You Specify

- Group size and standard (JIS, DIN, BCI, SAE)
- Voltage and CCA requirements
- Capacity (Ah) and reserve capacity
- Terminal type and polarity
- Case dimensions and hold-down
- Branding (labels, colors, packaging)

## OEM vs ODM vs Private Label

- **OEM** — you provide the specification; the manufacturer builds to it
- **ODM** — the manufacturer offers existing designs you can brand
- **Private Label** — you apply your brand to a manufacturer's product
