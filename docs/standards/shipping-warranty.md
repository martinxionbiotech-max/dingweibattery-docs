# Shipping & Warranty

Shipping automotive batteries (particularly lead-acid) involves specific handling, documentation, and regulatory requirements. Understanding these before ordering prevents delays and compliance issues.

## Shipping Considerations

### Lead-Acid Classification
Lead-acid batteries are classified as dangerous goods for transport because of the sulfuric acid electrolyte and lead content.

### Shipping Requirements
- **Packaging** — batteries must be packaged to prevent short circuits and acid leakage
- **Documentation** — dangerous goods declaration and MSDS (Material Safety Data Sheet)
- **Handling** — upright orientation, terminal protection
- **Bulk shipping** — ocean freight is standard for high-volume orders

### Import & Customs
- Confirm your destination country's import requirements
- Batteries may require specific labeling or compliance certificates
- Working with an experienced freight forwarder is recommended

## Warranty

Warranty terms cover manufacturing defects under normal use and typically include:

- **Coverage period** — varies by product and market
- **Defect coverage** — manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship
- **Exclusions** — damage from misuse, overcharging, deep discharge, or improper installation

## Before You Order

Confirm with the manufacturer:
1. Shipping method and Incoterms
2. Dangerous goods documentation provided
3. Warranty terms and claims process
4. Destination-specific compliance requirements
