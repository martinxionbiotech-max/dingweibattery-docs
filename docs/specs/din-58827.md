# DIN 58827 Battery (DIN88)

The **58827** is a DIN **DIN88** group-size automotive battery — a 12V European-configuration unit used across a wide band of premium passenger and light-commercial vehicles. It bridges the mid-capacity European segment, sitting above the standard H5/H6 fitments and below the larger H8/DIN100 platforms.

The `58827` designation is the DIN/EN type reference for this case. You'll see it cross-listed under the aliases **DIN88**, **LN5**, and **H8**, because European batteries carry parallel naming across the DIN, EN, and the newer LN (light-normal) classification systems. See [Standards Comparison](standards-comparison.md) to understand why the same box can carry three labels.

## Key Specifications

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Standard | EN 50342 (DIN/EN) |
| Group Size | DIN88 / LN5 / H8 |
| Voltage | 12V (6 cells) |
| Capacity (C20) | 88 Ah |
| CCA (reference) | 680–850 A (EN, −18°C) |
| Reserve Capacity | Confirm on inquiry |
| Terminal Type | Standard Euro (recessed) — confirm polarity |
| Application | European passenger and light commercial vehicles, luxury SUVs |

CCA is shown as a **reference** and is test-method dependent. The DIN88 is rated on the EN/DIN basis (−18°C), which is *not* directly comparable to a JIS (−15°C) figure. Datasheets for this case range from ~680 A to ~850 A CCA depending on plate design and grade. Confirm the final value and its basis with the manufacturer. See [CCA Testing](../quality/cca-testing.md).

## Electrical Performance

The DIN88 is a high-load European starting battery. Its electrical profile balances strong cranking current against enough stored energy for luxury-vehicle accessory loads.

- **Capacity (C20)** — 88 amp-hours at the 20-hour rate.
- **CCA** — 680–850 A on the EN basis; higher-grade AGM or premium flooded variants sit at the top of that range.
- **Reserve Capacity (RC)** — confirm on inquiry; it shifts with the Ah rating and plate formula.
- **Charging characteristics** — European vehicles with smart charging (BMS-controlled alternators) expect the battery to accept charge on a specific voltage profile; confirm compatibility with the vehicle's charging strategy.

For European start-stop or high-accessory applications, the related [60038 (DIN100)](din-60038.md) is the step up in capacity and reserve.

## Dimensions & Tolerance

The DIN88 uses a standardised European footprint. Reference dimensions:

| Dimension | Reference value |
|---|---|
| Length | 353 mm |
| Width | 175 mm |
| Height | 190 mm |
| Total height (incl. terminals) | ~190 mm |

The 353 × 175 × 190 mm envelope is shared by several mid-size European cases, which is precisely why the DIN88, LN5, and H8 labels overlap. Confirm the exact case size — especially terminal height — against the tray and hood clearance, as recessed-terminal lids add height above the case top.

## Terminal Type & Polarity

The DIN88 uses the **standard Euro (recessed) terminal** layout. European recessed terminals sit flush inside the lid rather than as exposed posts, and they are dimensionally distinct from JIS posts and BCI/SAE top posts.

**Polarity must be confirmed on inquiry.** European batteries are produced in both right-positive and left-positive layouts, and the correct orientation is dictated by the vehicle's cable routing. Confirm terminal layout and polarity against the specific vehicle before ordering.

## Compatible Vehicles & Applications

The DIN88 is a common fitment across European luxury and large-platform vehicles. Typical applications include:

- **BMW** — 7 Series (2002–2015), X5 (2007–2018), X6 (2008–2019)
- **Mercedes-Benz** — S-Class (2006–2016), ML/GLE (2005–2018)
- **Audi** — A8 (2004–2017), Q7 (2006–2015), A6 higher-spec models (2011–2018)
- **Porsche** — Cayenne (2003–2017)
- **Volkswagen** — Touareg (2003–2016)
- **Land Rover / Range Rover** — Discovery 4 (2010–2016), Range Rover (2006–2018)

These fitments are a cross-reference guide, not a guarantee. Always confirm against the vehicle's original equipment specification and the manufacturer before ordering.

## DIN88 vs DIN100

The DIN88 (58827) and the DIN100 (60038) share the same 353 × 175 × 190 mm footprint and the L5 case class, but differ in capacity and cranking output: 88 Ah / ~680–850 A versus 100 Ah / 900 A reference CCA. Choose the DIN100 where the vehicle or aftermarket load demands the additional reserve and crank margin. See [60038 (DIN100)](din-60038.md).
