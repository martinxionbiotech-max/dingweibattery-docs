# DIN 60038 Battery (DIN100)

The **60038** is a DIN **DIN100** group-size automotive battery — a 12V high-capacity European unit built for premium vehicles, light-commercial fleets, and start-stop or high-electrical-load applications. It is the top platform in the standard European passenger-car size class before stepping into commercial-truck footprints.

The `60038` reference is the DIN/EN type designation for this case. Aliases include **DIN100**, **L5**, and the EN "light-heavy" class. The parallel naming (DIN, EN, LN) is normal for European batteries — the same box carries multiple labels depending on the convention in use. See [Standards Comparison](standards-comparison.md) for the mapping.

## Key Specifications

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Standard | EN 50342 (DIN/EN) |
| Group Size | DIN100 / L5 |
| Voltage | 12V (6 cells) |
| Capacity (C20) | 100 Ah |
| CCA (reference) | 900 A (EN, −18°C) |
| Reserve Capacity | Confirm on inquiry |
| Terminal Type | Standard Euro (recessed) — confirm polarity |
| Application | Premium European vehicles, light commercial, start-stop/high load |

CCA is a **reference** value, test-method dependent. The DIN100 is rated on the EN/DIN basis (−18°C), which does not compare directly to a JIS (−15°C) figure — there is no universal multiplier between them. Always confirm the final value and basis with the manufacturer before a product specification. See [CCA Testing](../quality/cca-testing.md).

## Electrical Performance

The DIN100 is chosen when a mid-size European battery runs out of reserve or cranking margin. It holds the highest capacity in the standard passenger-case class.

- **Capacity (C20)** — 100 amp-hours at the 20-hour rate.
- **CCA** — 900 A reference on the EN basis; premium and AGM grades push toward the top of the range.
- **Reserve Capacity (RC)** — confirm on inquiry; extended reserve matters for start-stop and high-accessory vehicles.
- **Start-stop & BMS compatibility** — vehicles with engine start-stop cycle the battery far harder than conventional cars, and their battery management system (BMS) expects a specific charge-acceptance profile. Confirm whether the target vehicle requires flooded, EFB, or AGM chemistry, and whether the BMS supports it. See [Battery Chemistry Comparison](../standards/battery-chemistry-comparison.md).

For a deeper look at how the DIN100's cranking and reserve metrics interact, see [Capacity & Reserve](../quality/capacity-reserve.md).

## Dimensions & Tolerance

The DIN100 uses the L5 European footprint — identical in length and width to the DIN88, but a slightly larger case envelope for the higher cell count.

| Dimension | Reference value |
|---|---|
| Length | 353 mm |
| Width | 175 mm |
| Height | 190 mm |
| Wet weight | ~23 kg |

Confirm the exact case size and terminal height against the vehicle. Recessed-terminal lids add height above the bare case, and the clearance between the terminal recess and the hood is often tighter than the length/width fitment.

## Terminal Type & Polarity

The DIN100 uses the **standard Euro (recessed) terminal** layout, distinct from JIS posts and BCI/SAE top posts. Recessed terminals sit flush inside the lid, which protects them from shorting but also constrains cable clamp choice.

**Polarity and terminal layout must be confirmed on inquiry.** European batteries ship in right-positive and left-positive configurations; the correct orientation comes from the vehicle's cable routing and hold-down, not from the group size alone.

## Compatible Vehicles & Applications

The DIN100 serves high-capacity European and large-platform applications:

- **Premium European sedans and SUVs** — large-engine platforms with heavy accessory loads.
- **Light commercial vehicles and vans** — fleet units needing reserve for refrigeration, telematics, or lift gates.
- **Start-stop vehicles** — where the battery must accept repetitive charge/discharge cycling; confirm EFB/AGM chemistry requirements.
- **High-electrical-load aftermarket builds** — vehicles fitted with aftermarket audio, winches, or auxiliary lighting.

As with all European cases, these are cross-reference fitments — validate against the original equipment specification before ordering.

## DIN100 vs DIN88

The DIN100 (60038) and DIN88 (58827) share the L5 footprint but not the electrical profile. The DIN100 steps up to 100 Ah and 900 A reference CCA versus the DIN88's 88 Ah and ~680–850 A. Pick the DIN100 where reserve capacity or cranking margin is the binding constraint; see [58827 (DIN88)](din-58827.md).
