# JIS 190H52 (N200) Battery

The **190H52** is a JIS **N200** group-size automotive battery — the largest, highest-capacity starting unit in the JIS N-series. Built for the most demanding cranking loads in large commercial vehicles and heavy machinery, it pairs a 200 Ah class energy reserve with peak cold-cranking output.

The model code follows JIS convention: `190` is the cranking-performance rating, and `H52` is the terminal-and-case code (H denotes the terminal profile, 52 the taller case class used for high-capacity N-series cells). Aliases include **190H52**, **N200**, and **CMF190H52**. See [Standards Comparison](standards-comparison.md) for how JIS maps against DIN and BCI families.

## Key Specifications

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Standard | JIS D 5301 (N-series) |
| Group Size | N200 |
| Voltage | 12V (6 cells) |
| Capacity (C20) | 200 Ah |
| CCA (reference) | 1100 A (SAE, −18°C) |
| Reserve Capacity | ~407 min (confirm on inquiry) |
| Terminal Type | JIS post — confirm polarity on inquiry |
| Application | Large trucks, buses, construction/agricultural equipment |

As with every CCA figure on this site, the 1100 A value is a **reference** and is test-method dependent. A JIS-basis (−15°C) number and an SAE/EN-basis (−18°C) number are not comparable, and some vendor datasheets rate the N200 as high as 1200 CCA. Confirm the basis and final value with the manufacturer before writing a specification. See [CCA Testing](../quality/cca-testing.md).

## Electrical Performance

The N200 is selected when a standard N150 is not enough — where the engine is larger, the cold-weather cranking window is harsher, or the accessory bank is heavier.

- **Capacity (C20)** — 200 amp-hours at the 20-hour rate, the highest in the JIS line.
- **CCA** — roughly 1000–1200 A depending on test basis and plate design. This is the figure that matters for large-displacement diesels turning over in cold conditions.
- **Reserve Capacity (RC)** — some N200 datasheets list RC around 407 minutes at a 25 A discharge; confirm the exact value against the specific batch.
- **Charging current** — high-capacity N-series units accept substantial bulk charge; confirm the recommended rate from the datasheet to avoid overloading the alternator on short duty cycles.

For the biggest engines, capacity and cranking current rise together — you can't trade one for the other. See [Capacity & Reserve](../quality/capacity-reserve.md).

## Dimensions & Tolerance

Case dimensions are confirmed against the production batch. Reference figures are approximately:

| Dimension | Reference value |
|---|---|
| Length | ~515–521 mm |
| Width | ~267–278 mm |
| Height | ~216–241 mm (varies with lid/handle configuration) |
| Wet weight | ~52 kg |

The N200 is heavier than the N150 by roughly 7 kg and sits in a larger tray. Note the wide height spread across datasheets — flat-head and handled variants differ materially — so always confirm the exact case envelope for your installation rather than trusting a single published height.

## Terminal Type & Polarity

The N200 uses **JIS posts**, the smaller tapered terminals standard on Japanese and Asian vehicles. They do not interchange with DIN recessed terminals or BCI/SAE top posts without an adapter.

**Polarity and terminal layout must be confirmed on inquiry.** The N200 ships in both right-positive and left-positive configurations, and a mismatch means cables will not reach or a reversed-connection risk at install. Confirm the layout against the vehicle's cable routing and hold-down.

## Compatible Vehicles & Applications

The N200 targets the largest commercial and industrial starting loads:

- **Large trucks, tractor heads, and heavy haulers** — long-haul and prime-mover platforms with high-displacement diesel engines.
- **Buses and coaches** — including units running heavy pneumatic door systems and continuous climate loads.
- **Construction & agricultural equipment** — large excavators, wheel loaders, bulldozers, and harvesters combining peak cranking torque with constant vibration.
- **Diesel generator sets** — where a 12V bank must deliver an assured cold start on demand.

Like the N150, the N200 is a fuel-engine starting battery. It is not a deep-cycle or lithium/hybrid unit, and repeated deep discharge will shorten its life.

## N200 vs N150

The N200 steps up from the N150 in every dimension that matters: 200 Ah versus ~160 Ah, ~1100 A versus ~1050 A reference CCA, and a larger, heavier case. Choose the N200 when the N150's capacity or cranking margin is borderline for the engine in question. See the [145G51 (N150)](jis-145g51.md) page.
