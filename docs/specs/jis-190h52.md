# JIS 190H52 (N200) Battery

The **190H52** is a JIS **N200** group-size automotive battery designed for the highest cranking demands in large commercial vehicles and heavy machinery.

## Key Specifications

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Standard | JIS (Japanese Industrial Standard) |
| Group Size | N200 |
| Voltage | 12V |
| Terminal Type | JIS posts (confirm polarity) |
| Application | Large trucks, buses, construction/agricultural equipment |

## Dimensions

Dimensions are confirmed per production batch. Contact the manufacturer for exact case dimensions matched to your vehicle's battery tray and hold-down system.

## Electrical Performance

- **CCA** — cold cranking amps, measured to the JIS test basis (−15°C). See [CCA Testing](../quality/cca-testing.md).
- **Capacity (Ah)** — amp-hour rating at the C20 rate, among the highest in the JIS line.
- **Reserve Capacity (RC)** — extended reserve for vehicles with high accessory loads.

## Application Notes

The N200 group size serves the largest commercial vehicle starting demands. Confirm terminal layout, venting requirements, and mounting hardware before specifying.
